Questions we hear a lot.

Is there a minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom packaging?

No. We have no minimum order quantity. You can order as few as 50 custom printed packages or as many as 10,000.

What does Pack Nation do?

We are a packaging design and printing company. Our team specializes in package design and brand design, and we print your packaging for you, so design and production happen in one place.

Can I use my own packaging design?

Yes. Bring your own design and we’ll print it, or let our designers create custom packaging and brand design for you.

How long does custom packaging design and printing take?
  • Design: about 4 to 5 days
  • Printing: about 5 to 7 days after design approval
  • Samples: fast, so you see how your packaging looks without a long wait
Will I see a sample before you print my packaging?

Yes, always. You review and approve your sample before we print the full order. Free samples depend on your plan:

  • Basic: 2 free samples per design
  • Premium: 4 free samples per design
  • Advance: 8 free samples per design
What materials can you print packaging on?

We print on paperboard, kraft paper, corrugated boxes, laminated pouches, kraft paper pouches, and more.

Do I own my packaging design?

Yes. Once your design is made, it belongs to you, and you can use it however you like.

How much does custom packaging cost?

Pricing depends on your project. Send us a message on WhatsApp and we’ll reply within 30 minutes with a quote.

How do I pay for design and printing?

Payment is split into two parts: 50% to get started and 50% when we finish. This applies to both design and printing orders.

Do you offer free delivery?

Yes, delivery is free.

What happens if there’s a printing mistake?

If the mistake is ours, we reprint your order for free.
